## What this document will cover

- Purpose and scope — a screening aid for learning, not a medical device
- Users — student, evaluator, curious visitor
- Functional requirements — upload a drawing, get a label, confidence and heatmap; compare models
- Non-functional requirements — response time, privacy (images are not stored), reproducibility
- Constraints — small public dataset, free hosting

This page fills in as the project moves forward. Start with the **Project Overview** for the full picture.
