- **Every morning:** weigh in after the loo, before food. Write it — don't judge it.
- **Every Sunday:** compare this week's average with last week's.
- **Also check:** sleep average, steps average, protein days hit, gym sessions, diet streak.
- **Stuck 2 weeks?** Take away 100 kcal *or* add 2,000 steps — not both.
- **Dropping >1%/week?** Add 100–150 kcal back — speed is costing muscle.
- **Every 2 weeks:** body scan, same scale, same time. Monthly: photos front / side / back in the same light.
