- **Incline 15% at 4 km/h ≈ 8 METs** — close to jogging, but low impact and easy to recover from.
- 30 min ≈ **240 kcal** above rest for 65 kg. Flat walking at the same speed ≈ 65 kcal.
- Do it **after lifting** (or on its own) so it doesn't eat into strength.
- Steps are the most underrated fat-loss tool: 10k steps ≈ 240 kcal for 65 kg.
- Your phone counts treadmill steps too — the Gym page subtracts them so nothing is counted twice.
- Don't hold the rails — it cuts the calorie burn a lot.
