- With 5.5 h sleep, **fat lost fell by ~55%** and **lean mass lost rose by ~60%** — on the same diet.
- Short sleep also raised hunger. (Small study: 10 people, 14 days — but the direction is clear.)
- **Targets:** 7.5–8.5 h in bed, same bedtime every night (±30 min), screens off 30 min before.
- Log sleep hours in Gym → Today. Under 7 h for a few days? Fix sleep before cutting more calories.
- Late-night studying counts as "lost sleep" too — plan the day so the 108-mala morning still has 7.5 h behind it.
