- **Phase 1 · Habits (days 1–28):** fixed sleep time, meals on time, protein every day, 10k steps. Small deficit (~250 kcal).
- **Phase 2 · Fat loss (days 29–70):** train harder, structured incline walks. Deficit ~450 kcal (~0.6%/week).
- **Diet break (days 71–77):** eat at maintenance for a week. Recover, reset hunger, keep strength (chapter 6).
- **Phase 3 · Finish (days 78–100):** precise food, sleep as priority #1. Deficit up to ~500 kcal — never past 1%/week.
- **Realistic finish line:** ~13–14% body fat with muscle kept — clean abs outline and a visible V-shape.
- Scan every 2 weeks, same time, same scale (Gym → Plan shows the dates).
