title: Diet breaks work
icon: ⏸️
summary: Two weeks on maintenance between dieting blocks led to more fat loss in a controlled trial.
updated: 2026-11-28
visual: matador
caption: Same total dieting time; the break group lost more fat and their metabolism slowed less.
sources:
  - title: Byrne et al. 2017 — Intermittent energy restriction, the MATADOR study (Int J Obesity)
    url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5803575
---

- In the MATADOR trial, men who dieted in **2-week blocks with 2-week maintenance breaks** lost more fat (12.3 vs 8.0 kg).
- Their **resting metabolism dropped less** — the body "fought back" less.
- It was a small study in men with obesity, so treat it as a strong hint, not a law.
- **How I use it:** one maintenance week at day 71–77, and a maintenance day if a week ever feels brutal.
- A break is *eating at maintenance* — not a cheat week.
