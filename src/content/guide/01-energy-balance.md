- **Calories out** = body at rest + daily movement + steps + training + digesting food (~10%).
- **Calories in** = everything you eat and drink — including chai, sauces and "just a bite".
- A **deficit** (out > in) burns stored fat. A **surplus** builds tissue (muscle *and* fat).
- **Quick estimate:** bodyweight × 30–33 = rough maintenance. It ignores how active you are, so the Gym page calculates it from your steps, lifting and incline walk instead.
- **The real number** comes from your scale: after ~2 weeks of morning weigh-ins + logged food, the system calibrates your true maintenance.
- No food is "fattening" by itself — the daily total decides.
