- **Priority muscles:** side delts (lateral raises), lats (pull-ups, pulldowns, rows), upper chest (incline press).
- **Volume:** ~10–20 hard sets per muscle per week — more weekly sets led to more growth in research.
- **Effort:** most sets 1–3 reps short of failure, 6–15 reps. Log weight × reps every session.
- **Progressive overload:** each week, beat last time by +1 rep or +2.5 kg on the same exercise.
- **Waist:** comes from fat loss (chapters 1–5), not from ab exercises alone.
- Keep legs in the plan — strong legs make the whole frame look athletic.
- 60 minutes: warm-up 8 min → main lifts 45 min → stretch 7 min. Then the incline walk.
