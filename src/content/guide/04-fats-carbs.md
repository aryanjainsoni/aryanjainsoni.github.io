- **Fat:** never below ~0.6 g/kg — hormones and recovery need it. 0.6–0.9 g/kg is a good range.
- **Carbs:** whatever calories are left. They fuel heavy sets and incline walks.
- **Gym days:** keep carbs higher around training (pre/post workout meals).
- **Rest days:** same protein and fat, carbs a little lower.
- **Fibre:** 30–38 g/day — dal, sabzi, fruit, oats, chana. Keeps you full in a deficit.
- Oil, ghee, butter and nuts add up fast: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 135 kcal.
