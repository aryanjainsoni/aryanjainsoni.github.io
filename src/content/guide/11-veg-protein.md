- **Highest protein per calorie:** whey (20 g/100 kcal), soya chunks (15), tofu (11), hung curd (10).
- **Paneer:** 18 g protein per 100 g, but 20 g fat — keep portions to ~100 g.
- **Easy daily stack (~140 g):** 1 scoop whey (24) · 200 g hung curd (20) · 40 g soya (21) · 150 g tofu or 100 g paneer (18–24) · dal ×2 (16) · milk (8) · rest from roti/rice.
- Pair dal with rice or roti — together they give a complete protein.
- Soak or sprout chana and moong — easier to digest, better minerals.
