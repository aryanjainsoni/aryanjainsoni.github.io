- **Building / maintenance:** ~1.6 g per kg bodyweight. **Cutting:** 1.8–2.2 g/kg. At 65 kg → **~120–145 g/day**.
- Split it over **4–5 meals of 25–40 g** — easier to hit than 2 huge meals.
- Hit protein first, then fill the rest of the day with carbs and fats.
- Veg sources that work: whey, soya chunks, tofu, hung curd, paneer, dal + rice, milk, curd (see chapter 11).
- Whey is just food in powder form — useful, not magic. Use it to close the gap, not to replace meals.
