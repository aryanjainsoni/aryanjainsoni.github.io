- **Target:** 0.5–1% of bodyweight per week. At 65 kg that is about **0.3–0.65 kg/week**.
- **In calories:** 0.5% ≈ 360 kcal/day deficit · 0.7% ≈ 500 · 1% ≈ 715 (1 kg fat ≈ 7,700 kcal).
- Leaner you get → go slower. Near 12–14% body fat, stay closer to 0.5%.
- **Red flags you're going too fast:** strength dropping week after week, always tired, bad sleep, constant hunger.
- Big "100-day" deficits (900–1,100 kcal) are for advanced people with a coach watching daily — not needed for a clean V-shape.
