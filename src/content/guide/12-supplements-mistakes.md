- **Creatine monohydrate:** 3–5 g daily, any time, no loading needed. Best-researched supplement for strength.
- **Whey:** convenient protein. **Caffeine:** black coffee before training can help.
- **Get tested first (vegetarians):** vitamin B12 and vitamin D. Supplement only if a doctor says so.
- **Skip:** fat burners, detox teas, "mass gainers" full of sugar.
- **Mistakes:** crash diets · weekend binges that erase the week · cutting sleep · not tracking · comparing to someone's 6%-body-fat reel.
- **Never:** water or salt manipulation to "look shredded" — dangerous and it doesn't build anything.
- *Not medical advice — check with a doctor before starting any supplement.*
